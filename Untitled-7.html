Find quick answers to frequently asked department questions

You can apply for an official transcript through the student dashboard under the "Academics" tab. Verification usually takes 3 to 5 business days, and you will receive an email confirmation once it is ready for collection.

Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible to appear for the semester examinations. Shortfalls due to medical reasons must be backed by official certificates submitted to the HOD.

Elective registration opens during the final two weeks of the current semester. Log into the portal, navigate to "Course Registration", and select your choices in order of preference. Seats are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis.

For any technical glitches, login issues, or portal bugs, please raise a ticket directly by emailing support.portal@college.edu or visiting the IT Support Desk in Block C, Room 102.
